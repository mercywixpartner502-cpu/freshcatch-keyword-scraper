    How scoring works

    Opportunity · Demand · Freshness · Saturation

    Freshness — how alive the name is right now: recency of buyer posts and news, age on the radar, presence in today's trending feed. Tiers enforce the freshness law: 🔥 hot week > 🌱 this month > ✅ under 5 months > 💤 dormant.

    Demand — buyer pull: Reddit posts asking for help/setup/hire with the name, chatter volume, news mentions, search-engine presence.

    Saturation — how crowded it already is: engines suggesting it, a Wikipedia article existing, age on the radar.

    Opportunity = (demand + freshness + name-anchored specificity) discounted by saturation. WINDOW OPEN = fresh demand, little competition — the moment to build a gig.
